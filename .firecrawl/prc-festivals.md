# Progressive Rock Festivals

**Directory of active progressive rock festivals**.

**Crescendo Festival**

[Crescendo Festival](http://www.festival-crescendo.com/) is an annual progressive rock festival held in France. The first Crescendo Festival took place in the “Palais des Congrès” in Royan ( in 1999. The Festival now takes place every summer at the “Esplanade du Concié”, outdoors, in the oceanfront at Saint‑Palais‑sur‑Mer (western France).

**ProgDay**

[ProgDay](http://www.progday.net/) is one of the world’s longest running progressive rock festivals. The festival takes place in September at Storybook Farm, Chapel Hill, in North Carolina, USA.

**RoSfest**

[Rites of Spring Festival (RoSfest)](http://rosfest.com/) is an annual progressive rock festival held in Gettysburg (Pennsylvania), USA.

**Seaprog**

[Seaprog](http://seaprogfest.org/) is an annual progressive rock festival held in Seattle, Washington state.

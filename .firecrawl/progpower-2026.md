## We would like to play @ PP Europe

The line-up for 2026 is not complete yet. We are currently talking to several bands to see if we can book them. But you can always show your interest in playing at ProgPower Europe in the future.

If you play in a band and you think your music fits our festival, feel free to send us your music. We listen to all submissions, but we cannot answer every band personally. If we are interested in your band for a future edition of the festival, we will contact you.
